Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Centsible (“we,” “our,” or “the app”) handles information when you use the Centsible personal finance and budgeting application. Please read it before using the app.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use and the information you enter, Centsible may handle the following information:

  • Account and profile information: information associated with your account, such as your display name and account identifier.
  • Financial information you enter: income sources, income amounts and frequency, expense names and daily amounts, savings amounts and notes, bill names, amounts, due dates and payment status, and one-time expense names, amounts and dates.
  • Support messages: information you choose to provide if you contact us for assistance.

Do not enter bank account credentials, card numbers, or other sensitive information unless a feature explicitly requires it. Centsible's budgeting records are based on the information you provide; they should not be treated as verified bank balances or professional financial advice.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to provide account access, save and display your budgeting records, calculate budget summaries and suggested savings, support bill and expense tracking, troubleshoot problems, maintain app security, and respond to support requests.

3. Storage and Service Providers

Centsible uses Supabase for backend services and database storage. Information you save may be transmitted to and stored by Supabase according to the app's configuration and Supabase's applicable terms and privacy practices. Access controls and security settings are used to protect stored information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate Centsible, or disclosed when required by law, to respond to valid legal requests, to protect users and the service, or to address security or fraud concerns.

5. Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as needed to provide the app and for legitimate operational or legal purposes. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account information and budgeting records by contacting us. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

6. Your Choices

Please keep your login credentials private and sign out on devices you do not control. You can choose what financial information to enter. If you want to request access, correction, or deletion of your information, contact us using the address below.

7. Children's Privacy

Centsible is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where parental consent is required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Centsible changes. We will update the “Last updated” date when we publish a revision. Please review this page periodically.
